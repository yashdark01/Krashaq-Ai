Common soybean pests in Madhya Pradesh require regular scouting after 30 days after sowing (DAS).

**Semilooper:** Defoliates leaves. Scout weekly. Follow KVK threshold before chemical spray.

**Girdle beetle:** Affects stems and pods in some seasons. Remove crop residues; use recommended seed treatment where advised.

**Whitefly:** Can vector viral diseases. Monitor undersides of leaves; avoid unnecessary broad-spectrum sprays that harm natural enemies.

**Integrated Pest Management (IPM):**
1. Cultural — timely sowing, field hygiene, balanced nutrition
2. Mechanical — hand collection for localized infestations where feasible
3. Biological — prefer biocontrol agents when KVK recommends
4. Chemical — only as last resort per local agriculture university advisory

Always confirm product label, waiting period, and dosage with the latest MP agriculture department bulletin.
