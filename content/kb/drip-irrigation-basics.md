Drip irrigation delivers water directly to the root zone, reducing evaporation loss compared to flood irrigation.

**When to prefer drip:**

- Horticulture, vegetables, orchards
- Water-scarce regions or limited electricity for pumping
- Sandy soils with high percolation

**Operating tips:**

- Irrigate early morning (6–8 AM) or evening (5–7 PM) in hot seasons
- Clean filters weekly; flush lines periodically
- Combine with mulching to reduce evaporation

**Maintenance:**

- Check emitter uniformity monthly
- Repair leaks promptly — small leaks waste significant water over a season

**Crop note:** Emitter spacing and flow rate depend on crop root zone and soil type — follow system designer or KVK recommendations for your field layout.
