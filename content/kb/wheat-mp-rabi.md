Wheat is a major rabi crop in Madhya Pradesh. Timely sowing and nitrogen management are critical for yield.

**Sowing window:** Typically October–November for most MP districts (adjust for late-sown zones).

**Key stages:**

- Crown root initiation — first critical irrigation
- Tillering — monitor nitrogen; avoid excess in dense crop
- Flowering / grain fill — terminal heat stress risk; ensure adequate moisture

**Pests & diseases to watch:**

- Yellow rust and brown rust — scout from tillering onward
- Termite in rainfed patches — seed treatment and field sanitation

**Best practices:**

- Use certified seeds and recommended seed rate
- Soil test before urea top dressing
- Weed management in first 30–40 days after sowing

Consult the local Krishi Vigyan Kendra or agriculture department for variety-specific recommendations for your district.
