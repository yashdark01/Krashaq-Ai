PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) provides income support to eligible landholding farmer families.

**Benefit:** Rs 6,000 per year paid in three equal installments of Rs 2,000 each (verify current amounts on the official portal before advising).

**Eligibility (summary):**

- Landholding farmer families with cultivable land
- Small and marginal farmers are primary beneficiaries
- Institutional landholders and certain excluded categories are not eligible

**Farmer actions:**

1. Register or verify records on the official PM-KISAN portal
2. Keep Aadhaar-linked bank account details updated
3. Correct land records with local revenue office if mismatched

**Important:** Never quote installment dates or amounts without checking the latest official notification. Direct farmers to the PM-KISAN helpline or nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra for verification.
